# Datasets

All numbers below come from `docs/phase1_report.md` (generated by `scripts/verify_phase1.py` on the full data).
"Unique" = jobs not flagged as duplicates (same title + employer + place + identical/near-identical text).

| Source | Jobs (unique) | Region / period | Text | Real fields | Main caveats |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **naukri** | 12,000 (11,948) | India (Pune ~1.2k rows) | **skills only** (7-9 tags) | title, company, location(s), experience range, skills | no description, salary, education or dates; multi-city rows split into `cities`; the generic tag "IT Skills" is dropped |
| **emscad** | 17,880 (17,042) | mostly US/UK, ads from 2012-14 | **full** (median ~146 words; 17,800 usable) | title, description, requirements, benefits, location, employment type, education, **function label (64%)**, industry, fraud flag | no company name (companies are *derived* from a hash of the profile text, flagged `name_is_derived`), no skills column, no dates; 866 postings are labelled fraudulent (`is_fraudulent`) |
| **mcf** | 20,298 (18,733) | Singapore, mainly May-Jun 2019 | **preprocessed** (lower-case, stop-words removed) | title, company, salary (86%), seniority, employment type, category (100%), min experience, skills (<=20 per job), posting date | cannot show original passages; `job_id` is not unique (1,147 rows carry "Recruiter"); SGD salaries; skill lists look capped at 20 |
| **linkedin** (sample) | 13,989 (13,989) | US/UK/CA/AU, 12-17 Jan 2024 | **skills only** until `attach_summaries.py` adds `job_summary` | title, company, location, level, skills (LLM-extracted by the dataset author), first_seen | skills are noisy (education, licences, clearance codes) - only tags the lexicon vouches for are kept; experience is a seniority band; 6 days only |
| **fixture_crawl** | 300 (299) | synthetic | full | everything | SYNTHETIC pages produced by this project to exercise the crawlers |
| **sample_synthetic** | 300 | synthetic | full | everything | SYNTHETIC offline demo data; generated *from the curated knowledge graph*, so it must not be used for evaluation |

Not loaded into `jobs` (used in later phases): **resumes** (Kaggle/Hugging Face "Resume Dataset", 2,484 resumes, 24 categories) are cleaned to `data/processed/resumes_clean.csv`
with a title-masked copy (the first line is the job title and would leak the class label).

## Knowledge sources
* **ESCO** (European Commission; free to use with attribution): 13.8k skills, 3,039 occupations, 66.6k essential + 60.8k optional occupation-skill relations, 5.7k skill-skill relations. `P_3.rdf` is the ESCO *data model* (OWL/SKOS) - used as the ontology the knowledge graph follows, it contains no data.
* **O\*NET 31.0** (US Dept. of Labor, CC BY 4.0 - attribution + note of modification required): 1,016 occupations, job zones, ~15 reported titles each, 12.4k occupation->technology edges (hot/in-demand technologies), 18.5k related-occupation edges.
* **Curated graph** (`ingest/curated_kb.py`): 20 roles, 24 skill hierarchies (the examples in the project brief).
