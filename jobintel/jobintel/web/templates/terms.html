Plain-language rules for using Jobintel.

What Jobintel is

Jobintel is a search and analysis tool over a stored collection of job postings. It is not an employer or a recruiter, it does not take applications, and it cannot promise that you will get an interview or a job.

Postings and accuracy

Postings come from public datasets and are a snapshot. A posting may be out of date, closed or wrong. Experience, salary and skills are read from the posting text or its data and can be incomplete or mis-read. Check the original posting before you act on it.

Matches and recommendations

A match explanation says why the software ranked a posting. It is a guide, not a judgement of your suitability or of the employer.

Your account

Keep your password private and give accurate details. Do not use another person’s account. You can stop using Jobintel at any time.

Use of the site

Do not try to break, overload or probe the site, collect its pages in bulk, or use it to send spam or harm others. The operator may limit or remove access to protect the site and its users.
